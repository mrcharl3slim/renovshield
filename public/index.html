The problem

The risk doesn't end when you sign

First-time homeowners commit tens of thousands of dollars to decisions they can't judge, then have to manage the works around a full-time job.

Quotes are hard to compare

Different formats, unfamiliar terms and missing items make it hard to tell if two firms offer the same work.

Payments run ahead of the work

Milestone payments are often asked for before you can check that the work is finished properly.

Agreements get lost

Changes agreed on WhatsApp are hard to find later, which leaves you weak in a dispute or at handover.
